How it works

Start a session. Tap Win. That's the whole thing.

Scores, court, session type and notes are all optional. The only required tap is Win or Loss — so logging never pulls you out of the game. Mistyped something? Edit or delete any game later.

Sessions group your games the way you actually play: one Tuesday open play, one league night, one drilling hour — each with its own record.

CoreMotion, on the watch

We count your swings. Here's what it can't do.

Your Apple Watch reads the accelerometer while you play and counts swings live, on the watch. The raw motion is analysed as it arrives and thrown away when the session ends — only the count is kept.

  • Ball handling and arm movement between points can read as swings, so the count runs a little high.
  • It does not know a dink from a drive, or a swing from a whiff.
  • Treat it as an honest measure of how much you played — not a laser count of paddle contacts.

Telling shot types apart needs motion-shape analysis. That's post-launch work, and we would rather say so than imply we already do it.
