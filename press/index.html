Boilerplate

One sentence: PickleRecord is a pickleball match tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch that turns individual games into a season-long win–loss record, with no account and no data leaving your device.

One paragraph: Most pickleball apps keep score during a game. PickleRecord keeps your record across a season. Log each game in two taps from your iPhone or Apple Watch and the app builds your win rate, streaks, and month-over-month trends from it — with unlimited match history free forever. The Apple Watch app counts swings from wrist motion on-device and saves sessions to Apple Health as pickleball workouts. There is no account system, no server, and no analytics: the App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected. PickleRecord is made by TJNAI.ai LLC, a small studio in North Carolina.
